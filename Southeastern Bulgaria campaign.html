01 — Main Issues

What's going wrong

Four interconnected problems that have pushed residents out and held back growth across Burgas, Sliven, Stara Zagora, and Yambol.

Population
–12%

125,000 people left in 10 years

Young people move to Sofia or Western Europe. In Yambol, the population replacement rate has fallen to just 60.1%.

Energy
Coal

Maritsa East is running out of time

Thousands of jobs depend on lignite coal, but EU clean energy rules will end that era — with or without a local plan in place.

Employment
46pt

Education shapes who finds work here

83% of university graduates are employed. For those with only primary education it drops to 37%. That gap defines who can stay.

Rural Access
Rural

Villages left behind while cities grow

Poor broadband, aging roads, few local jobs. Rural areas in Yambol and Sliven have seen decades of underinvestment.

03 — Opinion
May 2026

Our region is shrinking.
It doesn't have to.

District Governor
Regional Development Council

Between 2011 and 2021, the Southeastern Region lost 125,000 people. Young people leave for Sofia, for Berlin, for anywhere that seems to have a future. That isn't a natural trend — it's the result of decisions that weren't made and investments that didn't come.

"The Maritsa East coal jobs will change whether we plan for it or not. We are choosing to plan."

That means solar and wind capacity at Maritsa East. It means railways connecting all four provinces. Broadband for every village. Startup hubs so that young people here have a real alternative to leaving.

€700M to €1B in phased investment — drawn from EU funds, the Bulgarian state, and private sector partnerships — for every worker, family, and community across this region.
